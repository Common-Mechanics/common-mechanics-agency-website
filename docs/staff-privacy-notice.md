# Privacy notice for team members

**Common Mechanics Ltd** · company no. 17385700 · 71–75 Shelton Street, London, WC2H 9JQ
**Questions or requests:** privacy@commonmechanics.io
**Last updated:** 3 September 2026

This explains what Common Mechanics does with personal data about the people who work here.
It's separate from the public notice at commonmechanics.io/privacy, which covers site
visitors. UK GDPR Art. 13 requires us to give you this.

Give this to everyone on the team, and to new people during onboarding.

---

## What we publish about you on the website

The Team section of commonmechanics.io shows, for each team member:

- your name
- your work email address
- a link to your LinkedIn profile
- the city you're based in (city only — not an address)

**Why:** clients hiring a small studio want to know who they'd actually be working with, and
to be able to reach you directly. Publishing this is ordinary professional identification for
a services business.

**Lawful basis:** legitimate interests (UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) — ours, in being a credible and
contactable studio.

**Not consent.** That's deliberate and it works in your favour: consent would have to be
freely given, which is difficult to guarantee between an employer and a team member. Relying
on legitimate interests instead means you keep a real **right to object** under Art. 21. If
you object, we stop unless we can show compelling grounds that override your objection — and
for a name on a website we almost certainly can't. In practice: ask, and it comes down.

**One consequence worth knowing.** A published `mailto:` address gets scraped, so expect
spam to the address on the site. Tell us if it becomes a problem and we'll change how it's
displayed.

## What else we hold about you

- **Employment or contractor records** — the contract, right-to-work documentation, and
  whatever HMRC and payroll require. Kept for as long as the law requires (generally 6 years
  after the end of the tax year in which the engagement ends).
- **Work correspondence and project records** — your email, chat and repository activity in
  the ordinary course of doing the work. Kept for 6 years from the end of the relevant client
  engagement, in line with our client retention schedule.
- **Access credentials** — accounts on Cloudflare, GitHub, the mail provider and similar,
  which necessarily record who did what.

We don't monitor your device, track your location, or use automated decision-making about
you.

## Who else sees it

Our processors — the mail provider, the hosting and code platforms listed in
`data-protection.md`, our accountant, and HMRC where we're legally required to report. Nobody
else, and we don't sell or share anything for marketing.

## When you leave

Your details come off the website **within 30 days** of your last day. That's a step on the
leavers checklist rather than something you should have to chase, but if you spot yourself
still on the site after 30 days, email privacy@commonmechanics.io and it'll be fixed
immediately.

Employment and financial records stay for the statutory periods above — we can't delete those
early, and that's a legal obligation rather than a choice.

## Your rights

You can ask us to:

- **give you a copy** of what we hold about you (a subject access request)
- **correct** anything that's wrong
- **delete** it, where we don't have a legal obligation or overriding reason to keep it
- **restrict** or **object to** how we use it — including, as above, having your details taken
  off the website
- **hand it over** in a portable format

Email privacy@commonmechanics.io. We'll respond within one month; if a request is complex we
can extend that by two months, and we'll tell you if we do. There's no charge.

If you're not happy with how we handle it, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's
Office — ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or 0303 123 1113. We'd rather you came to us first, but
you don't have to.
